Personal Health Data App

This is a personal, single-user application operated by its owner for private use only. It is not a public service and has no other users.

The application reads the owner's own Google Health data (such as weight, exercise, sleep, and nutrition) through the Google Health API in read-only mode, solely so the owner can track their personal health and habits.

For details on what data is accessed and how it is handled, see the Privacy Policy.
