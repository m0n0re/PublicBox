Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Overview

This application is a personal, single-user tool operated by its owner for private, non-commercial use. It is used only by the owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the application accesses the owner's own Google Health data through the Google Health API, using read-only scopes only. This may include activity and fitness data, health metrics and measurements (such as weight), sleep, nutrition, profile, settings, location, electrocardiogram (ECG), and irregular rhythm notification data.

How the data is used

The data is used solely for the owner's personal health tracking, such as recording daily weight, exercise, and calorie intake, and keeping personal habit records. The data is never used for advertising, marketing, or any form of profiling of other people.

Storage

Data retrieved through the API is processed and stored only on computers owned and operated by the owner. OAuth tokens are stored locally in files with restricted permissions. No data is uploaded to any third-party server by this application.

Sharing

The data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

Retention and deletion

The owner may revoke the application's access at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings, which immediately stops any further access. Locally stored copies of the data can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the GitHub repository that hosts this page: https://github.com/m0n0re/PublicBox
